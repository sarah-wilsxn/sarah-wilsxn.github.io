Summary

In this paper, we introduce Shaperd, a real-time traffic shaper built to help fully encrypted protocol-based tools stay harder to detect in censoring environments. Instead of requiring major redesigns, Shaperd is meant to be practical to adopt and works directly on packet contents to shape traffic behavior on the fly. Its constraint-based approach lets users tune traffic flows to match desired features, which can help defend against modern passive analysis and active probing techniques used by censors. Early results are encouraging: Shaperd adds only minimal throughput overhead while improving resilience.
